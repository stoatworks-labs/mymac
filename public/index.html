⌘ mymac

Make a borrowed Mac behave like yours, then put it back.

Someone else's show machine with the Dock on the left, tap-to-click off and Quick Note in a hot corner? Pick your settings once, then run one line in its Terminal. mymac saves every value before it changes it, and one command puts the Mac back exactly as you found it. No install, no admin password.

  1. 1 · Read your own Mac

    Run this on your Mac. It only reads, and opens this page with your settings filled in.

  2. 2 · Tune it for the show

    Change anything below, and add the show-safety settings. The address bar always holds your profile, so bookmark it.

  3. 3 · On the show Mac

    Paste your one-liner into Terminal. Before you leave, put everything back:

    bash ~/.mymac/restore.sh
